The laptop picture shown in the home page's hero (src/components/Hero.astro).

laptop.png (1536 x 1024, transparent background) is a composite made for this site in November 2026:

- the laptop body was generated with ChatGPT image generation by the project owner (November 2026), on a green
  screen that was then keyed out;
- the screen is a real capture of the desktop app (1920 x 1080, Arabic, dark theme) composited onto it: the
  fictitious "Unpaid loan" sample with its legal grounds drafted by the local model gemma3:4b, the review box
  ticked, 9 of 9 mandatory elements present. Every word and number on the screen is the app's own.

The one image serves both languages and both themes and is never mirrored. The site builds AVIF and WebP
renditions of it (480, 720, 960 and 1440 px wide) with the transparency kept.
