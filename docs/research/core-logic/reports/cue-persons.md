# The cue verbs in every person (18 November 2026)

**What the owner asked:** "The «لا ننفي» gap is not only in the challenge check: the reader's cue table has the same blind
spot, and real lawyers write in the 'we' and 'I' forms. Extend the cue table so every cue verb is listed in all persons and
both genders (singular and plural), generated by a rule from the verb, not from any challenge note. Measure the change on
the Codex half of dev only, as the handbook requires, and keep it if it does not lower any line."

**The change** (`src/DzLegal.CoreLogic.Reader/Cues/CueEngine.cs`):
- Each cue verb is written once, as its stem.
- Every form is generated by two rules, `CueEngine.Present` and `CueEngine.Perfect`.
- **Dispute verbs, in the present** (the table's tense):
  - I «ا…», we «ن…», you and she «ت…», he «ي…»;
  - you (f.) «تـين»;
  - the duals «ـان»;
  - the masculine plurals «ـون»;
  - the feminine plurals «ـن»;
  - the forms after «لم» and «لن» («ـوا», «ـا», «تـي»).

  A stem ending in «ي» drops it before «و» and in «تـين»: «ينفون», «ينفوا», «تنفين».
- **Refusal verbs, in the perfect** (the table's tense), every person: «امتنعت», «امتنعنا», «امتنعوا», «امتنعتم», «امتنعتن», «امتنعن», and the duals.
- Every form of the old table is still there; a test checks this.
- No challenge note was used to write the rule. The challenge check's own matching of 18 November (af59add) is removed: it now reads the same table, which keeps one list of cues for the reader and the checks.

**The measure** (`reader cue-votes`, new):
- **Notes:** the tuning notes, the Codex half of dev, `exports/dev-tune-2`: 32 notes, 86 gold events.
- **Method:** for every gold event, the table's vote over the note's four fields (no vote when fields disagree), against the gold polarity.

**No line moved, so by the owner's rule the change is kept.** These 32 notes hold no "we" or "I" form of a cue verb, so the
measure cannot show a gain either. The gain is in the forms lawyers write, which the tests now cover. Files:
`dz-core-research\reader\runs\cue-persons\before.json` and `after.json`.

**What the measure shows besides.** Five of the 18 votes are wrong, before and after alike. None of them involves a cue
verb's person:
- n-dev-00031: `employment_started` is stated in the gold; the vote says denied.
- n-dev-00043: `payment_made` is not mentioned in the gold; the vote says stated.
- n-dev-00059: `notice_served` is disputed in the gold; the vote says stated.
- n-dev-00079: `notice_served` is disputed in the gold; the vote says stated.
- n-dev-00099: `rent_unpaid` is denied in the gold; the vote says stated.

On these notes the cue vote is reliable only for stated events (14 of 15 right). Rung 2's rule makes the reader abstain
where cues and model disagree, so a wrong vote costs coverage, not a wrong answer. These five are for the cue table's own
tuning, on the tuning notes, as a separate change.

**One more hazard, read from the code, not measured:** dispute verbs are matched as substrings of the clause, so «تنفي» is
also found inside «تنفيذ» (execution). That is a separate change, to be measured on its own.
