01 · A case outline

Nine ordered rows replace the pills. Stable numbers, a slim gold current-section marker and separate completion icons keep the navigation calm and scannable.

02 · Paper is for the petition

A4 proportions, Arabic Naskh type, deliberate paragraph spacing and printed headings. Blue-grey field tools sit outside the sheet; PDF, Word and watermark controls share the Edit/Preview row.

03 · Glass around the work

The glass option adds tinted layers, edge highlights and gentle depth to the surrounding panels. Paper, fields and legal warnings remain opaque. This static study uses no live backdrop blur.
