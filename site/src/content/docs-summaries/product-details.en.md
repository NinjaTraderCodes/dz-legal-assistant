- The supported input is formal Arabic, the language the petition must be in (CPCA art. 8); notes in Darija or mostly in French are still accepted, never refused or cut.
- 1,361 articles are loaded: the complete Family Code, the complete CPCA with its 2022 amendment, 33 articles of the Civil and Commercial Codes and 17 labour-law articles; none of these checks is a lawyer's review.
- The identification, the court, the facts, the dates, the signature block and the requests come from the form and your own lines; the model writes only the legal grounds.
- The analysis reads the facts, gathers the candidate articles from the catalogue without any model, and lets the model choose among them; nothing is ticked until you confirm the claim type.
- In the grounds, the code writes each article and its one-line rule; the AI writes only how the facts meet it, and an answer that cites an article or has a number found nowhere in your notes is asked again, then left as «(...)», which blocks export.
- Each AI-written section carries «راجعت هذا القسم وأتحمل مضمونه»; PDF, Word and copy are refused until every one is ticked and no «(...)» remains, and editing a section does not count as reviewing it.
- The analysis takes about 2 to 3 minutes on a laptop without a graphics card (117 to 181 s measured on an Intel i5-1335U); with no model installed, the template mode builds the draft from the templates and your notes.
- The claim catalogue (its claim types, articles and required flags) is not yet verified by a lawyer: an independent audit found errors in it, and corrections are under way.
