- The app quotes an article only from its local copy of the official Arabic texts, never from a model's memory: a 4-billion-parameter model cannot recall article numbers or wording reliably.
- 1,361 articles are loaded today: the complete Family Code (227) and the complete CPCA (1,084), 33 articles of the Civil and Commercial Codes, and 17 labour-law articles.
- Each text says how it was checked: read against the Official Journal page that published it, proved letter by letter against its official PDF, or transcribed from page images and read twice.
- No article is marked verified until a lawyer has checked it against its page.
- Nothing is scraped, searched online or read from PDFs while you work; the analysis starts only when you click.
- Under each suggested article: the official reference, its place in the code, a link to the official document, and a "not verified by a lawyer" tag.
- With no recognised claim type there is no candidate and nothing is proposed; a small model does not use this way out, so the lawyer judges the proposed claim type.
- The app never downloads anything: importing a text is a maintainer's task at development time, and the shipped app reads its texts offline.
