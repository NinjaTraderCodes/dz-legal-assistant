# Open legal questions

**The owner's standing rule of 15 November 2026.** The owner is not the legal reviewer, and there is none. A question of
legal reading (which reading of the law is right) is never sent to the owner: the record keeps the protective behaviour
it has (it warns, keeps both readings, never blocks), the item stays at grade C or D, and the question is kept here with
its readings and what each would change, for a legal reviewer if the product ever has one. Each unit's section is
written by `unit.py questions <unit>`; entry 4 of each question is written by the executor from the readings only, and
kept while the readings do not change.

Every check behind these entries except Codex's support check was made by Claude models (HANDBOOK, section 5b).

### formal-notice 1. Does the statement of claim, or its official service, stand in the place of the warning? (o1, grade C; items p1, p2, p3)

1. **The question:** Does the statement of claim, or its official service, stand in the place of the warning? «هل تقوم عريضة افتتاح الدعوى أو تبليغها الرسمي مقام الإنذار؟»
2. **The readings:**
   - The officially served petition stands in the place of the warning. (rests on b5)
   - A separate notice before the claim is required. (rests on b1, b2)
   - The Supreme Court's reading (decision 1009298 of 10-09-2015, commercial and maritime chamber): notice is in principle not needed for successive or continuing obligations, except one notice at the failure to perform the first of them; once that is met, the lawsuit stands as the notice; the lawsuit does not replace a notice the contract requires. (rests on b5, b36)
3. **What the record does (the protective behaviour it keeps):** Treat a prior notice as required and warn of its absence; do not block the export.
4. **What would change with each answer:** If the served petition stands in the place of the warning: p1, p2 and p3 would no longer warn that a prior notice is missing. If a separate notice is required: they warn when it is missing, as today. On the Supreme Court's reading: they would ask whether the obligation is successive or continuing and whether one notice was served at the first failure, and warn only when it was not. No reading makes any of them block.

### formal-notice 2. What is the postal way the code refers to, and does a registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt suffice? (o2, grade D; items p1, p2, p3, n1, doc2)

2. **The readings:**

   - Post suffices only in a form set by a text not found. (rests on b5)
3. **What the record does (the protective behaviour it keeps):** Accept a registered letter, keep the acknowledgement of receipt and check its form; a bailiff's warning is the safer means.

If post suffices only in a form set by a text not found: n1 warns on a postal notice and p1, p2 and p3 do not count it as done, as today for a means not listed.

### formal-notice 4. Is notice required for damages for late payment of a sum of money, and from what date do they run? (o4, grade C; items p1, b15)

2. **The readings:**
   - Notice is required under article 179, and damages run from the notice. (rests on b1, b15)
   - Article 186 is a special text that replaces notice, running from the claim. (rests on b15)
3. **What the record does (the protective behaviour it keeps):** Warn about notice whenever damages are asked, and state both the notice date and the filing date.
4. **What would change with each answer:** If article 179 applies: p1 warns about the notice whenever damages for late payment are asked, as today. If article 186 replaces the notice: p1 would say nothing about the notice for that request, and the message would state that the damages run from the claim (b15).

### formal-notice 6. Is a claim for a sum of money, for delivery, for the return of a thing or for eviction from leased premises a claim for performance that needs notice under articles 119 and 164? (o6, grade C; items p3, n8)

1. **The question:** Is a claim for a sum of money, for delivery, for the return of a thing or for eviction from leased premises a claim for performance that needs notice under articles 119 and 164? «هل تعد دعوى المطالبة بمبلغ نقدي أو بالتسليم أو برد شيء أو بإخلاء العين المؤجرة مطالبة بالتنفيذ تستلزم الإعذار وفق المادتين 119 و164؟»
2. **The readings:**
   - Yes: every claim for performance in kind, the return of leased premises included. (rests on b2, b4)
   - No: article 164 concerns compelled performance after judgment, and eviction follows the lease rules. (rests on b4)
   - No, for a lease whose term has ended: it ends at the term without a notice to quit and the tenant must return the property when it ends, so no formal notice is needed to claim eviction. (rests on b27, b28)
3. **What the record does (the protective behaviour it keeps):** Ask about the notice and warn when it is missing; never block.
4. **What would change with each answer:** If every claim for performance needs notice: p3 asks about the notice and warns when it is missing for payment, delivery, return and eviction, as today. If article 164 concerns performance after judgment only: p3 would say nothing for these claims. If a lease that has ended needs no notice: p3 would say nothing for eviction after the term, and n8 would carry that case.

### formal-notice 7. Which means stand in the place of a warning, and who judges it? (o8, grade C; items n1, p1, p2, p3)

   - The lawyer judges whether the means stands in the place of a warning, and the app is silent once he says it does. (rests on b5)

3. **What the record does (the protective behaviour it keeps):** Always warn when another means was used.
4. **What would change with each answer:** If the lawyer judges the means: n1 would ask whether the means used stands in the place of a warning and be silent once the lawyer says it does. If every other means is flagged: n1 warns whenever a means other than a bailiff's warning or the post was used, and p1, p2 and p3 do not count it as done, as today.

### formal-notice 8. Does a notice served on the day of the petition, or after it, suffice? (o9, grade C; items p1, p2, p3, doc1, n12)

1. **The question:** Does a notice served on the day of the petition, or after it, suffice? «هل يكفي إعذار بلغ في يوم رفع الدعوى أو بعده؟»
2. **The readings:**
   - The notice must precede the filing, even for a claim for damages alone, since compensation is due only after notice. (rests on b2, b1)
   - For damages, a notice before the judgment suffices. (rests on b1)
3. **What the record does (the protective behaviour it keeps):** Warn when the notice was served on the petition's day or after it, and ask when that is not known (n12); the messages and the first document ask to check that the notice date precedes the petition.
4. **What would change with each answer:** If the notice must precede the filing even for damages: p1, p2 and p3 warn when the notice was served on the petition's day or after it, and n12 asks when that is not known, as today. If a notice before the judgment suffices for damages: for a claim for damages alone, p1 would count a notice served after the petition as done and n12 would say nothing; p2 and p3 would not change. Case bc7 is this question.

### formal-notice 13. Does an electronic statement (e-mail, text message) count as a written declaration of non-performance that dispenses with notice? (o14, grade C; items b10, doc4, n1)

1. **The question:** Does an electronic statement (e-mail, text message) count as a written declaration of non-performance that dispenses with notice? «هل يعد التصريح الإلكتروني (بريد إلكتروني، رسالة نصية) تصريحا كتابيا بعدم التنفيذ يغني عن الإعذار؟»
2. **The readings:**
   - An electronic statement whose author and integrity can be checked is writing. (rests on b10)
   - Only a paper writing counts. (rests on b10)
3. **What the record does (the protective behaviour it keeps):** The lawyer judges; keep the electronic message and proof of its source; a notice remains the safer course.
4. **What would change with each answer:** If an electronic statement whose author and integrity can be checked is writing: such a statement would exempt from notice (b10), p1, p2 and p3 would say the step is not needed, and doc4 would ask for the proof of its source. If only paper counts: an electronic statement would not exempt, and the steps warn when no notice was sent.
