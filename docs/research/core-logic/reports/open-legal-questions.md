# Open legal questions

**The owner's standing rule of 15 November 2026.** The owner is not the legal reviewer, and there is none. A question of
legal reading (which reading of the law is right) is never sent to the owner: the record keeps the protective behaviour
it has (it warns, keeps both readings, never blocks), the item stays at grade C or D, and the question is kept here with
its readings and what each would change, for a legal reviewer if the product ever has one. Each unit's section is
written by `unit.py questions <unit>`; entry 4 of each question is written by the executor from the readings only, and
kept while the readings do not change.

Every check behind these entries except Codex's support check was made by Claude models (HANDBOOK, section 5b).

### formal-notice 1. Does the statement of claim, or its official service, stand in the place of the warning? (o1, grade C; items p1, p2, p3)

1. **The question:** Does the statement of claim, or its official service, stand in the place of the warning? «هل تقوم عريضة افتتاح الدعوى أو تبليغها الرسمي مقام الإنذار؟»
2. **The readings:**
   - The officially served petition stands in the place of the warning. (rests on b5)
   - A separate notice before the claim is required. (rests on b1, b2)
   - The Supreme Court's reading (decision 1009298 of 10-09-2015, commercial and maritime chamber): notice is in principle not needed for successive or continuing obligations, except one notice at the failure to perform the first of them; once that is met, the lawsuit stands as the notice; the lawsuit does not replace a notice the contract requires. (rests on b5, b36)
3. **What the record does (the protective behaviour it keeps):** Treat a prior notice as required and warn of its absence; do not block the export.
4. **What would change with each answer:** If the served petition stands in the place of the warning: p1, p2 and p3 would no longer warn that a prior notice is missing. If a separate notice is required: they warn when it is missing, as today. On the Supreme Court's reading: they would ask whether the obligation is successive or continuing and whether one notice was served at the first failure, and warn only when it was not. No reading makes any of them block.

### periods 7. Does the interruption of limitation survive if the proceedings lapse, the claim is dismissed for form, or the plaintiff withdraws it; and is a formal notice served by a bailiff, or a registered letter claiming the debt, an interrupting command to pay? (o8, grade C; items b18, b44, doc2, d1, d2, d3, d4, d6, d7, d8)
