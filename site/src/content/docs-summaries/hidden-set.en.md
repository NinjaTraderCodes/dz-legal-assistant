- The hidden set measures how well the app reads the facts, on cases its developers have never seen.
- The 61 development cases were used to build and tune the reader, so its score on them is optimistic; the hidden set is the honest measure.
- It is written by someone who has not worked on the reader, and who may not read its prompts, the development cases or the benchmark results; it never enters the repository and is never sent to the developers: only its summary is shared.
- About 100 fictitious cases in formal Arabic: 4 or 5 for each of the 21 claim types of the catalogue, and 10 outside it.
- The cases read like a lawyer's notes: side details, several dates and amounts, facts that did not happen and facts one party denies.
- The first run (30 October 2026) got 32 of 90 cases fully right with the claim type given; on 31 October 2026, Nemotron 3 Ultra online got 64 of 90 with the claim type given, and 60 of 90 with the claim type read, the claim type right on 85 of 90 and all 10 out-of-scope cases declined.
- A second set by an independent author (8 November 2026, 90 cases, the claim type given): the local Gemma 4 E4B got 58 of 90 fully right in 21 s per case, against 39 of 90 in 28 s for the previous local model, Gemma 3 4B, with the same reader.
- Once its results have been looked at, a set is no longer hidden: the next measure of a changed reader needs a new set.
