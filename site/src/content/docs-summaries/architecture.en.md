- The desktop app and the web demo share everything below their shells; only the shell and the model provider differ, and the desktop never references the hosted providers.
- The formal check is a single pure function with no input or output, no AI and no clock: deterministic, and fast enough to run on every keystroke.
- Legally critical identification data comes straight from the form, through templates; the model writes only the narrative sections, which limits invention.
- Each line of your facts notes becomes a «حيث إن» paragraph: small models reversed who lent and who borrowed when they rewrote facts.
- The model's text is checked against your material: foreign script, numbers found in no input, numbers from your notes that it dropped, stray Latin words, text mostly not in Arabic.
- These checks cannot detect errors of meaning, such as swapped roles: those remain the lawyer's review.
- Each mandatory element carries its article, a severity and a check from a small closed set; anything new must be a new kind with unit tests, never logic hidden in the interface.
- Deterministic before AI: the articles a claim type requires are proposed by the catalogue, not by the model, and quotations are checked against the article's text.
